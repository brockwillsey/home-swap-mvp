# Product Brief: home-swap-mvp

## Executive Summary

home-swap-mvp is a membership-based home exchange platform positioned as an art residency network, designed for remote workers, second-home owners, and creatives seeking affordable, low-friction travel within a trusted community. The platform combines the ease of Airbnb's booking experience with Home Exchange's reciprocal model and Soho House's curated membership approach.

Members pay an annual fee and can book stays through three flexible options: direct home swaps, points-based exchanges for non-simultaneous stays, or direct booking with a fee. The art residency positioning attracts quality members who value experiences, while partnerships with local creative studios (printmaking, pottery, etc.) offer optional enrichment programs.

The core value proposition is simple: nice homes, easy booking, trusted community, with creative experiences as a bonus.

---

## Core Vision

### Problem Statement

Vacation accommodations are expensive, and existing solutions force travelers to choose between affordability and ease. Traditional home exchange platforms like HomeExchange offer cost savings through reciprocal stays, but require users to send dozens of messages before successfully booking—creating significant friction that discourages use. Meanwhile, platforms like Airbnb have normalized staying in others' homes but remain transactional, expensive, and lack genuine community.

### Problem Impact

For remote workers with location flexibility and second-home owners willing to share their spaces, this friction represents a missed opportunity. These users have both the means (homes to offer) and the motivation (desire for affordable, authentic travel) but lack a platform that makes exchanges easy and trustworthy. The result: they either overpay for Airbnb rentals or abandon home exchange after frustrating messaging experiences.

### Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

- **Airbnb/VRBO**: Easy to book but purely transactional. High fees, no community, no reciprocity option.
- **HomeExchange**: Right concept, wrong execution. Messaging-heavy process creates friction that kills conversion.
- **Soho House**: Curated community and trust, but focused on commercial spaces, not homes. Extremely expensive.

No platform combines easy booking, reciprocal exchange options, and a curated community of like-minded people.

### Proposed Solution

A membership-based platform that offers:

1. **Frictionless booking** - Select available dates and book directly, no messaging required
2. **Flexible exchange models** - Direct swaps, points-based exchanges, or pay-to-stay options
3. **Curated membership** - Application and referral-based admission ensures community quality and trust
4. **Art residency positioning** - Attracts creatives and experience-seekers; partnerships with local studios offer optional programs
5. **Annual membership model** - Creates commitment and filters for serious participants

### Key Differentiators

1. **"Airbnb meets Home Exchange meets Soho House"** - First platform to combine easy booking, reciprocal stays, and curated community
2. **Art residency positioning** - Unique market angle that naturally attracts quality members and interesting homes
3. **Trust through curation** - Application/referral process builds community trust that transactional platforms cannot replicate
4. **Network effect moat** - The community is the product; first-mover advantage in a niche creates defensibility
5. **Timing** - Remote work normalization, second-home growth, and Airbnb's cultural shift have primed the market

## Target Users

### Shared User DNA

All members share a "global citizen creative" mindset: culture-curious, experience-seeking, and art-appreciating. They value authentic travel over tourist experiences, appreciate well-designed spaces, and believe in community over transactions.

### Primary Member Types

#### 1. The Home Exchanger

**Profile:** "Maya & Daniel" - A couple in their 40s with a primary home in Chicago and a second home in Bacalar, Mexico. Both work remotely in creative industries (she's a UX designer, he's a writer). They travel 4-6 times per year and want to maximize their second home's value while accessing interesting places to stay.

**Needs:**
- Easy way to list multiple properties with separate availability calendars
- Exchange access to their homes for stays elsewhere without cash outlay
- Trust that guests will respect their spaces
- Flexibility between direct swaps and points-based exchanges

**Pain Today:** HomeExchange requires too much back-and-forth messaging. Airbnb means paying for stays while their second home sits empty.

**Success Moment:** Booking a month in Lisbon by exchanging points earned from hosting in Bacalar - no cash, no endless messages, great guests.

---

#### 2. The Traveler

**Profile:** "James" - A 35-year-old product manager who rents an apartment in Austin. Works remote-flexible (2-3 weeks per month location-independent). Loves exploring new cities for 1-2 week stretches but finds Airbnb expensive for longer stays.

**Needs:**
- Affordable stays longer than typical hotel visits
- Option to offer his apartment for exchange OR pay when that's not practical
- Vetted, trustworthy community (not random Airbnb hosts)
- Interesting locations with character, not generic rentals

**Pain Today:** Airbnb costs add up for frequent travel. Hotels feel sterile. Subletting his apartment is complicated.

**Success Moment:** Spending three weeks in Mexico City for less than one week would cost on Airbnb, staying in a home that feels curated, not commercial.

---

#### 3. The Artist-in-Residence

**Profile:** "Sofia" - A 29-year-old ceramicist based in Portland. Building her practice, limited budget, dreams of working in new environments with access to different studios and techniques. May or may not have a space to exchange.

**Needs:**
- Affordable access to art residency experiences
- Studio partnerships (pottery, printmaking, etc.) in destination cities
- Community of fellow creatives to connect with
- Flexibility - some trips are about art, some just about living somewhere new

**Pain Today:** Traditional residencies are competitive and expensive. Airbnb + studio rental separately is cost-prohibitive.

**Success Moment:** A month in Oaxaca with a place to stay AND access to a local ceramics studio, all through one membership.

---

#### 4. The Art Patron

**Profile:** "Richard" - A 58-year-old semi-retired architect in Santa Fe with a guest casita. Doesn't travel much anymore but loves being part of creative communities. Has disposable income and wants to support emerging artists.

**Needs:**
- Way to participate without being an active traveler
- Support artists through sponsorship or hosting
- Community connection and belonging
- Bring interesting creative people to his local community

**Pain Today:** No easy way to combine philanthropy with community membership. Traditional art patronage feels distant and transactional.

**Success Moment:** Sponsoring Sofia's residency in Santa Fe, hosting her in his casita, introducing her to local gallery owners, feeling part of her artistic journey.

---

### Secondary Users

#### Studio Partners

**Profile:** Local creative studios (printmaking shops, pottery studios, photography darkrooms, co-working spaces) that offer add-on programs to traveling members.

**Value Exchange:**
- **Free membership** - no cost to sign up as a studio partner
- **They offer:** Add-on programs, workshops, studio access (similar to gym membership add-ons)
- **They get:** Out-of-town customers, exposure to traveling creatives, potential collaborations

**Example:** A letterpress studio in Mexico City offers a 3-day printmaking intensive that members can book as an add-on to their home stay.

---

### User Journey

**Discovery:** "It's like Airbnb but a private community where you can exchange your home for longer stays."

**Application:** Prospective member applies with brief profile - who they are, what they'd offer (home, art, patronage), why they want to join. Referrals fast-track approval.

**Onboarding:**
1. Complete profile and verify identity
2. List home(s) with photos, description, availability calendar
3. Set preferences: swap-only, points, or cash options
4. Browse community - homes, members, upcoming studio programs

**First Booking:** Search by destination and dates → see available homes → book directly (no messaging required) → confirm exchange type (swap/points/cash)

**Aha Moment:** First stay in a thoughtfully designed home, greeted by a welcome note from the owner, optional pottery workshop booked for the weekend - "This is nothing like Airbnb."

**Long-term:** Platform becomes default for travel planning. Member builds reputation, accumulates points, develops relationships with repeat exchange partners. Some become patrons. Some host artists. Community deepens.

## MVP Scope

### Core Features (MVP)

**Member Management:**
- Member signup with application form
- Manual approval process via email confirmation
- Basic member profiles (bio, photo, location, creative interests)
- Stripe integration for $300/year membership payments

**Home Listings:**
- Create/edit home listings with photos and descriptions
- Availability calendar management (per property)
- Support for multiple homes per member
- Location-based listing with city/region

**Search & Discovery:**
- Search homes by location and date range
- View available homes with photos and details
- Filter by availability that matches desired dates

**Booking System:**
- Direct booking - select dates and book (no negotiation required)
- Two booking types: direct swap OR points exchange
- Points system: earn points when hosting, spend when traveling
- Booking confirmation and status tracking

**Messaging:**
- In-app messaging between members
- Pre-booking inquiries (optional, not required to book)
- Post-booking coordination (arrival details, etc.)
- General community messaging (relationship building)

**Platform:**
- Web application (mobile-responsive design)
- Architecture supports future mobile app transition
- Stripe payment processing for memberships and points

---

### Out of Scope for MVP

**Deferred to v1.1:**
- Cash/pay-to-stay booking option
- Studio partner listings and programs
- Studio add-on booking
- Reviews and ratings system

**Deferred to v2.0+:**
- Native mobile applications (iOS/Android)
- Automated approval workflows
- Patron sponsorship features
- Advanced matching algorithms
- Other space types (studios, boats, co-working)

**Intentionally Excluded:**
- Complex negotiation flows (contradicts core value prop)
- Public-facing listings (members-only community)
- Instant automated approval (curation is the point)

---

### MVP Success Criteria

**Validation Gates:**

| Criteria | Target | Signal |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Members join and pay | 5 members at 3 months | People will pay for this |
| Homes get listed | 80%+ of members list homes | Supply side works |
| Bookings complete | 2 bookings at 3 months | Core loop functions |
| Positive experience | Both parties satisfied | Trust model works |
| Retention signal | Members stay engaged | Community has value |

**Go/No-Go Decision Point:**
At 3 months, if we have 5+ paying members, 2+ completed bookings with positive feedback, and members actively using messaging - proceed to v1.1 with cash bookings and studio partnerships.

**Kill Criteria:**
If after 3 months we can't get 5 paying members OR completed bookings have negative feedback indicating trust/quality issues - revisit core assumptions before scaling.
